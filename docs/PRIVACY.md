# Chat Saver privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Chat Saver is a browser extension that saves your ChatGPT and Claude chats to your own computer.

## What the extension accesses

- **Your ChatGPT and Claude chats and files**, when you click Save chat, choose chats in the popup, import an export ZIP, or turn on automatic backup. It reads them from chatgpt.com and claude.ai using your existing login, in your own browser.
- **Your settings**: the AI provider, model and API key you enter. They are kept in your browser's extension storage and never leave it, except that your key is sent to the provider you chose when it writes a summary.

## Where your data goes

- Saved chats are written to your Downloads folder. A copy of each transcript is kept in your browser's local storage so you can search them in the library. The developer of Chat Saver never receives them.
- If you turn on the AI summary, the text of the chat being saved is sent to the provider you picked (OpenAI or Anthropic), using your own API key, so it can write the summary. That provider's own privacy policy applies to that request.
- Nothing else is sent anywhere. There are no analytics, tracking, or ads, and no Chat Saver server.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't use your data for anything except saving your chats as you asked.
- We don't collect personal information.
